How the numbers are worked out, and which columns are read
  • A call counts as a sale when Disposition is SOLD, or a Closed / Call Closed column says yes.
  • Close rate = sales ÷ billable calls. If the upload has no Billable column, every call counts.
  • Cost per sale = billable calls × the dollar price in the pricing tier name (or a Cost column if one exists) ÷ sales.
  • Annual premium per $1 = monthly premium sold × 12 ÷ call cost.
  • ROI = 9-month advance (average monthly premium sold × 9, at 100% commission) ÷ cost per sale. Same as total monthly premium sold × 9 ÷ call cost.
  • CPA trend: est. spend ÷ sales in each period. Weeks start Monday. Campaigns are grouped by name (Backup, Limitless, OG), combining their $35 and $40 tiers.
  • Carrier called in uses a Carrier Called In or Target Carrier column. Carrier sold uses the Carrier column.
  • Google carrier uses the Google Carrier column, with spellings of one carrier grouped (AmAm, AMAM and American Amicable count as one; AIG and American General count as Corebridge; entries naming several carriers count as Multiple carriers).
  • Without a Billable column, a call counts as billable when its Call Status is COMPLETED.
  • Columns are matched by name. Campaign uses Campaign, then Pricing Tier, then Lead Source. Reason uses Reason for Call, then Tags. A Reason for Call Code column adds its own chart, and a Notes column shows in the sales table.
  • Times are converted to US Eastern.
  • Call length comes from the Duration column. Drop-off rates (under 2, 5, 10 and 60 sec) are shares of calls that have a duration; calls that never connected have none and are left out.
  • Best hour = highest close rate among hours with at least 20 billable calls, so one lucky call can't win it.
