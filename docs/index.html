01

Enroll in your pre-licensing course

Required training before the state exam.

Free enrollment code FFLAGENTFORCE-2A83JS9
  1. Open the official course site and enter the code above when prompted. It makes the course free.
  2. Choose your resident state.
  3. Select Life Only and proceed to checkout.
  4. Create a new student account. Only the required fields are needed.
  5. Watch for the welcome email with instructions to begin right away.
The course times out after 30 days. Start as soon as your welcome email arrives.

Get fingerprinted

The process depends on your state.

Open the link below and find the fingerprinting process for your state.

05

Buy your state license

Do this after you pass your state exam.

Once you pass the exam with 70% or better, purchase your Life Only license in your resident state. Your NPN is issued after your license is processed.

Questions about the application? Ask your hiring manager before you submit.
