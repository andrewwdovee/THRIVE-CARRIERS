# Lead Tech Marketing Services Agreement

Business-to-Business Marketing, Lead Generation & Technology Services

This Marketing Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into as of the Effective Date below by and between Lead Tech (“Lead Tech,” “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) and the client identified below (“Client,” “you,” or “your”).

## 1 · Client information

Client / Legal Business Name, Authorized Representative, Email Address, Phone Number and Effective Date are as given on the onboarding form and in the execution block below.

## 2 · Purpose and scope of services

Lead Tech provides marketing, advertising, lead-generation, inbound-call, recruiting-advertising, software, automation, technology, and related marketing services. Services may include, without limitation:

- Lead generation and lead acquisition;
- Inbound calls and call-generation campaigns;
- Advertising campaigns on third-party platforms;
- Recruiting advertisements and recruiting funnels;
- Social-media and Instagram-related marketing tools or software;
- Landing pages, portals, forms, websites, funnels, and inbound systems;
- Marketing automation, CRM integrations, tracking systems, and workflows;
- Ad creatives, copy, keywords, targeting strategies, campaign structures, and related marketing assets; and
- Other services specifically described in an order, proposal, invoice, statement of work, or written communication accepted by the parties.

The specific services, quantities, campaign budgets, setup fees, recurring fees, and other commercial terms may be set forth in an invoice, proposal, order form, statement of work, or other written agreement incorporated into this Agreement.

## 3 · Marketing budgets and third-party platform spend

Client understands and acknowledges that amounts designated for advertising, lead acquisition, inbound calls, software, media placement, platform fees, or other third-party services may be committed or spent by Lead Tech on Client’s behalf.

Such funds may be paid to, deposited with, or otherwise committed to third-party advertising platforms, publishers, lead vendors, software providers, call providers, technology providers, or other service providers.

Once Lead Tech has incurred, committed, allocated, or spent funds in connection with Client’s campaign or requested services, those funds may be non-refundable to the extent they have been spent or are non-recoverable by Lead Tech.

Lead Tech will not be responsible for refunds resulting from a third party’s policies, platform decisions, rejected advertisements, lead quality disputes, account restrictions, outages, or other matters outside Lead Tech’s reasonable control, except to the extent otherwise expressly agreed in writing.

Client authorizes Lead Tech to use the applicable marketing budget for the services purchased and acknowledges that marketing results are not guaranteed.

Unless expressly stated in writing, Lead Tech does not guarantee any particular number of leads, calls, appointments, recruits, sales, revenue, return on advertising spend, or other business result.

## 4 · Payment; no improper chargebacks or payment disputes

Client agrees to pay all invoices, deposits, setup fees, recurring fees, campaign budgets, and other amounts when due.

Client acknowledges that Lead Tech may immediately begin work, reserve advertising inventory, purchase leads, commit third-party resources, create technology, or incur other expenses after payment or authorization.

Client agrees not to initiate or encourage a fraudulent, deceptive, or improper chargeback, payment reversal, or payment dispute for services that Lead Tech has provided, commenced, purchased, reserved, committed, or performed.

Before initiating any payment dispute concerning an amount that Client genuinely believes is erroneous, Client agrees to notify Lead Tech in writing and provide Lead Tech a reasonable opportunity to investigate and resolve the issue.

Nothing in this Agreement is intended to waive or restrict any non-waivable rights or remedies that Client may have under applicable law or the rules governing the applicable payment method.

If a chargeback or payment reversal is initiated in violation of this Agreement and Lead Tech incurs fees, penalties, administrative costs, or other losses as a result, Lead Tech may seek recovery of those amounts to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## 5 · Client responsibilities

- Client will provide accurate information, approvals, credentials, brand materials, and other items reasonably necessary for Lead Tech to perform the services.
- Client is responsible for ensuring that information supplied to Lead Tech is accurate and that Client has the right to use any materials, trademarks, images, testimonials, data, or other content supplied to Lead Tech.
- Client will timely review and approve advertisements, landing pages, scripts, creative materials, and other deliverables when approval is requested.
- Client will comply with applicable laws, regulations, advertising rules, platform policies, licensing requirements, and industry requirements applicable to Client’s business and offers.

## 6 · Ownership of Lead Tech intellectual property

Except for Client-owned materials expressly identified in writing, all intellectual property, systems, processes, technology, know-how, campaign structures, templates, methodologies, proprietary information, and other materials created, developed, licensed, or supplied by Lead Tech remain the property of Lead Tech or its licensors.

This includes:

- Ad creatives, graphic designs, videos, images, copy, scripts, headlines, and marketing concepts;
- Keywords, targeting methods, campaign structures, audience strategies, and optimization methods;
- Landing pages, funnels, portals, forms, websites, dashboards, automations, workflows, integrations, and software configurations;
- Software, code, prompts, systems, databases, templates, documentation, processes, and technical architecture;
- Lead-routing systems, inbound-call systems, tracking systems, and proprietary marketing infrastructure; and
- Lead Tech’s methods, strategies, know-how, trade secrets, and other proprietary business information.

Payment for services does not transfer ownership of Lead Tech’s proprietary systems, software, templates, methods, campaign architecture, or intellectual property unless Lead Tech expressly agrees to such transfer in a separate written instrument.

## 7 · Limited license to client

Subject to Client’s full and timely payment and continued compliance with this Agreement, Lead Tech may provide Client with a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable license to use certain deliverables solely for Client’s internal business and marketing purposes during the applicable service period.

This license does not authorize Client to sell, license, distribute, copy, reverse engineer, reproduce, recreate, or provide Lead Tech materials to another marketing provider for duplication.

## 8 · Non-replication; protection of proprietary marketing systems

During the term of Client’s relationship with Lead Tech and for twelve (12) months after the end of the applicable services, Client will not knowingly, directly or indirectly, use Lead Tech’s confidential or proprietary materials to recreate, duplicate, replicate, reverse engineer, or develop a substantially similar system for Client or for another person or business.

This restriction applies to proprietary ad creatives, campaign structures, keyword strategies, targeting systems, inbound portals, call-routing systems, software configurations, automations, workflows, funnels, templates, technical systems, and other proprietary materials provided by Lead Tech.

Client also agrees not to provide such materials to a competing agency, contractor, developer, marketing company, or other third party for the purpose of reproducing substantially similar proprietary systems.

This section does not prohibit Client from using general knowledge, independently developed materials, or information that is lawfully available to the public without breach of this Agreement.

Nothing in this Agreement restricts conduct that applicable law does not permit the parties to restrict.

## 9 · Confidentiality

Each party may receive confidential or proprietary information belonging to the other party.

The receiving party will use reasonable care to protect such information and will not disclose it to third parties except to employees, contractors, professional advisers, or service providers who have a legitimate need to know and are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

Lead Tech’s confidential information includes, without limitation, pricing structures, vendor information, lead sources, campaign data, software architecture, credentials, customer lists, marketing methods, campaign performance information, technical processes, and other non-public business information.

## 10 · Leads, data, and personal information

Lead Tech and Client will handle lead information and personal information in accordance with applicable law and the parties’ respective obligations.

Client is responsible for determining whether its intended use of leads, calls, messages, advertisements, and consumer information complies with applicable federal and state laws, regulations, consent requirements, telemarketing requirements, privacy requirements, and platform rules.

Lead Tech may use third-party providers to provide advertising, calling, software, hosting, analytics, CRM, lead distribution, and other services.

Client acknowledges that third-party platforms may have their own terms, privacy policies, restrictions, and data practices.

## 11 · Third-party platforms and service providers

Lead Tech may use third-party platforms and vendors in performing the services.

Lead Tech is not responsible for a third party’s downtime, policy changes, account suspension, advertising rejection, algorithm changes, pricing changes, technical failures, data loss, or other actions outside Lead Tech’s reasonable control.

Lead Tech may substitute comparable vendors or technology when reasonably necessary to perform the services.

## 12 · No guarantee of results

Client acknowledges that marketing and advertising performance depends on numerous factors outside Lead Tech’s control.

Unless expressly guaranteed in a written statement signed by Lead Tech, all forecasts, estimates, projections, expected lead volumes, expected call volumes, and performance expectations are estimates only and are not guarantees.

## 13 · Term and termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with the applicable service terms or by either party upon written notice, subject to any minimum term, campaign commitment, or cancellation terms stated in an applicable order, proposal, or statement of work.

Termination does not eliminate Client’s obligation to pay amounts already due or amounts already committed or incurred for Client’s services.

Sections concerning payment obligations, intellectual property, confidentiality, non-replication, limitations of liability, indemnification, dispute resolution, and other provisions that by their nature should survive termination will survive.

## 14 · Indemnification

To the extent permitted by law, Client agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Lead Tech and its owners, officers, employees, contractors, and agents from claims, losses, damages, liabilities, penalties, costs, and reasonable attorneys’ fees arising out of Client’s products or services, Client-provided materials, Client’s violation of applicable law or platform rules, or Client’s misuse of leads, consumer information, software, or marketing materials.

## 15 · Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Lead Tech will not be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages, including lost profits, lost revenue, lost business opportunities, or loss of data arising from or related to the services.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Lead Tech’s aggregate liability arising from the Agreement will not exceed the amount actually paid by Client to Lead Tech for the specific services giving rise to the claim during the three (3) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## 16 · Dispute resolution and governing law

The parties will first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through direct written communication.

This Agreement will be governed by the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to conflict-of-law principles, unless applicable law requires otherwise.

Any court proceeding permitted under this Agreement will be brought in a court of competent jurisdiction in Florida, unless the parties later agree otherwise in writing.

Nothing in this section prevents either party from seeking temporary or emergency equitable relief where necessary to protect confidential information, intellectual property, trade secrets, or other proprietary rights.

## 17 · No waiver; severability

A party’s failure to enforce any provision of this Agreement does not waive that party’s right to enforce the provision later.

If any provision is determined to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions will remain in effect, and the unenforceable provision will be modified only to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, to the extent permitted by law.

## 18 · Entire agreement; amendments

This Agreement, together with any applicable proposal, invoice, order form, statement of work, or written service terms incorporated into it, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter and supersedes prior discussions or understandings concerning that subject matter.

Any amendment must be in writing and accepted by both parties.

## 19 · Electronic signatures

The parties agree that electronic signatures, digital signatures, scanned signatures, and electronically accepted versions of this Agreement may be used and will have the same legal effect as original signatures to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## 20 · Acknowledgment

By signing below, Client acknowledges that Client has read and understands this Agreement; has had an opportunity to ask questions or seek independent legal advice; understands that marketing funds may be spent or committed to third-party platforms and vendors; understands that marketing results are not guaranteed; and agrees to comply with the payment, confidentiality, intellectual-property, and non-replication provisions of this Agreement.

# Thrive Companies Agency Agreement

This agreement is between **{{ENTITY}}** ("Thrive", "we", "us") and the person
named on the form ("you"), and it covers writing under the agency. It sits
alongside the Lead Tech Marketing Agreement above; where that one is about
buying calls, this one is about the agency relationship.

## 1 · Your debt is yours

**An advance is a loan against commission you have not earned yet. If the policy
charges back, lapses, or never pays, the advance comes back — and it comes back
from you.**

**(a) A negative balance is a debt.** If chargebacks take your account below
zero, that balance is yours. We set it off against future commission first. If
you stop writing, move agencies or leave, whatever is left is payable on demand.

**(b) Rolling it up does not clear it.** When a carrier rolls an unpaid balance
up the hierarchy — to your upline, or to Thrive — the money moves but the
responsibility does not. You still owe it. Thrive, or whoever absorbed it, may
recover it from you directly and may set it off against any commission, bonus,
override or other payment owed to you, now or later.

**(c) You will not roll debt up on purpose.** That means not writing business you
expect to charge back, not writing on people who cannot or will not pay, and not
walking away from a negative balance for the upline to absorb. Doing any of those
is a breach of this agreement, and the balance becomes immediately due in full.

**(d) What it costs to collect.** If we have to chase a balance, you also cover
the reasonable costs of collecting it, including legal fees.

## 2 · What belongs to Thrive

Everything the agency puts in your hands stays the agency's: scripts, rebuttals,
training, recorded calls, the dialer build and its routing, lead sources and what
they cost, carrier terms and commission levels, the software and its templates,
the artwork, and anything else Thrive made or paid to have made.

You may use it while you are with Thrive, and not after. You will not copy it,
keep it, publish it, sell it, or take it to another agency, IMO or FMO. Anything
you produce for Thrive in the course of the work belongs to Thrive.

## 3 · No recruiting from inside

**While you are with Thrive, and for twelve (12) months after you leave, you will
not recruit or solicit any agent, producer or staff member of Thrive Companies to
another agency, IMO or FMO — and you will not help anyone else do it.**

**What it costs.** For each person you recruit or solicit in breach of this, you
will pay Thrive **$5,000**, plus the reasonable costs of enforcing it. That figure
is a genuine pre-estimate of what it costs the agency to replace and retrain
somebody — the recruiting, the licensing, the onboarding, the production lost in
between — and not a penalty. The actual loss is hard to measure, which is exactly
why a figure is agreed up front.

**What this does not stop.** A general advertisement that is not aimed at Thrive's
people, and somebody who comes to you entirely on their own without any approach
from you or anyone acting for you.

## 4 · Confidentiality

Agent lists and contact details, commission levels, carrier terms, lead costs,
client and applicant data — all confidential, while you are here and after you
leave. Client data also has its own rules under law, and nothing here reduces
them.

## 5 · General

Governed by the laws of the State of {{STATE}}. Sections 1 to 4 carry on after
you leave. If a court finds any part of this unenforceable — the twelve months,
or the $5,000 — it may reduce it to what is enforceable rather than striking it
out, and the rest stands.
