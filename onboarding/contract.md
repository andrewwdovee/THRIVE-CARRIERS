# Inbound Programme Agreement

This agreement is between **{{ENTITY}}** ("Thrive", "we", "us"), which operates
the Lead Tech inbound call programme, and the person or business named on the
form ("you"). It takes effect on the date you sign it.

## 1. What you are buying

Thrive buys advertising, answers the calls that advertising generates, and
transfers qualified callers to you. Depending on what you have ordered that may
include:

- **Google Calls Subscription** — inbound calls from paid search, routed to your line.
- **Inbound Final Expense Transfers** — live transfers from the inbound desk, to a daily cap.
- **Individual Call** — a single transferred call, billed on its own.
- **Recruiting Ad Campaign** — a campaign run on your behalf to recruit producers.
- **Instagram Software** — access to the messaging software and its templates.

What you have ordered, its price and its billing cycle are recorded on your
account and on your Stripe receipts. Those records control.

## 2. Ad spend is spent

**Most of what you pay is advertising spend, and it is paid out to Google and
the other ad platforms within hours of your payment clearing. Once it is spent
it is gone. It cannot be recalled by Thrive, by you, by your bank, or by
Google.**

**(a) Ad spend is non-refundable.** Once a campaign has run, the money behind it
is not recoverable and will not be returned, in whole or in part — including on
a decision to stop, a change of mind, a change in your licensing, poor call
outcomes, or your own close rate.

**(b) You agree not to charge back ad spend.** You will not start, and will not
ask anyone else to start, a chargeback, payment dispute, ACH return or reversal
against Thrive for any charge that is or includes advertising spend. If you
think you have been charged in error you will raise it with us first, in
writing, and give us ten (10) business days to put it right.

**(c) If a chargeback is filed anyway.** It is a breach of this agreement, and
we may suspend call delivery and every other service on your account at once,
set the disputed amount off against any wallet balance or credit you hold, and
recover that amount together with the card network's dispute fee and our
reasonable costs of collection. The account stays suspended until the balance
is settled.

**(d) This is not a waiver of fraud protection.** Nothing here asks you to give
up a dispute over a charge you did not authorise, a duplicate charge, or a
charge for something never delivered. Those are section 4, and we refund them.

## 3. The inbound portal rules

1. **The wallet runs the delivery.** Calls are delivered against a funded
   wallet. At zero, delivery stops. Topping up restarts it. We do not deliver
   on credit.
2. **Payment is due the day it is raised.** An invoice unpaid after 12 hours is
   past due, and a past-due account is paused: routing off for a subscription,
   spend paused for a campaign.
3. **You take the calls.** Transfers come during the hours you agreed. A call
   you do not answer is still delivered and still billable. Tell us before your
   hours change.
4. **Licensing is yours to keep current.** Accept transfers only in states where
   you hold an active, appointed licence. Tell us the same day one lapses and we
   will stop routing those states.
5. **Caps and coverage are set in writing.** Your daily transfer cap and
   coverage area are recorded on your account. Ask for a change; never assume
   one we have not confirmed.
6. **Calls are recorded**, for quality and for settling disputes. Taking
   transfers is your consent to that, and you confirm your own use of the
   recordings follows the law in the states you work.
7. **The callers are yours to work, not to sell.** You may not resell, share,
   syndicate or re-transfer them to another agency, agent or lead buyer.
8. **One account, one agency.** Your login and your routing are yours. Do not
   share them.
9. **Do not contact the ad platforms about our campaigns.** The ad accounts are
   ours. Contacting Google about them, or complaining against them, is a breach
   of this agreement.
10. **Changes and cancellation.** Cancel a subscription in writing before the
    next billing date. Cancelling mid-cycle stops delivery; ad spend already
    committed for that cycle is not returned (section 2). Pro-rata credits are
    section 4.
11. **Raising a problem.** Email the fulfilment desk. We log it, listen back and
    come back to you, and every credit decision is recorded on your account.

## 4. What we do refund

Refunded or credited in Stripe, against the recorded account:

- **An individual call** that was mis-transferred, silent, duplicated, or
  outside your agreed states or hours — once we have listened back and
  confirmed it.
- **A cancelled membership**, pro-rata from the cancellation date, for the part
  of the fee that is not ad spend.
- **Service quality**, where we agree with you that we did not deliver what was
  ordered.
- **A duplicate charge**, in full, as soon as both charges are visible.
- **Goodwill**, at our discretion.

Raise a call credit within seven (7) days of the call. After that the recording
may have aged out of retention and we cannot verify it.

## 5. Your details, and your photo

Keep your contact details, licensed states and hours current on the account.

If you are an agent with Thrive Companies, you will give us a current headshot,
and we may use it in Thrive Companies marketing — agent highlights,
leaderboards, social posts — for as long as you are with the agency. Ask us and
we will stop using it. If you are not with the agency, no photo is needed and
none is kept.

## 6. General

Governed by the laws of the State of {{STATE}}. This is the whole agreement
between us about the inbound programme and it replaces anything said
beforehand. If a court finds part of it unenforceable the rest stands. We may
change these rules on thirty (30) days' notice; taking calls after that is
acceptance.
