Program rules

Inbound call rules

These apply to every inbound call you take. Calls are reviewed every day.

The first rule
All inbounds are sold through Thrive carriers.

Calls are reviewed on a daily basis. Selling a deal through an external contract leads to a suspension from all LeadTech marketing.

Suspensions for selling outside Thrive

Chargebacks mean a lifetime ban. If you purchase calls, take the calls, and then dispute the charge with your bank, you will be terminated from the inbound portal for life.

Wallet funds are wiped during a suspension. If you're suspended with money in your wallet, all funds are removed from the wallet at the end of the week.

All wallets are wiped every Saturday night and reset to $0. The one exception is the 50-calls-per-week package, whose wallet carries over as long as next week's subscription goes through.

Be live by the time you selected. If you're not marked as live in the LeadTech inbound portal by the start time you picked on the sheet, your portal is disabled for the rest of the day.

Returns & credits

Inbound refund policy

Refunds depend on where the call came from. Google calls can earn refunds for bad calls. Meta and TV calls can't be refunded.

Google calls

Refunds available
✕✕ → ✓ 1 refund

For every 2 bad calls you get per day, 1 refund is added to your account.

What counts as a bad call
  • An agent called in for customer supportNot a consumer on the line.
  • The call was dead airNo one was on the other line. The agent must be located in the USA for this to apply.

Meta / TV calls

No refunds

There are no refunds on Meta or TV calls. These calls have a buffer so you don't get billed for dead-air calls.
